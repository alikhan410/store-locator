# StoreTrail App Listing

## App Introduction

StoreTrail is a comprehensive store locator app that helps merchants display their physical store locations to customers through an interactive map interface. The app enables customers to easily find nearby stores, get directions, and view store details, helping merchants drive foot traffic to their brick-and-mortar locations.

## App Details

StoreTrail solves the challenge of connecting online customers with physical store locations by providing a customizable store locator that integrates seamlessly into Shopify stores. The app helps merchants increase foot traffic by making it easy for customers to find nearby locations, view store details, and get directions through an intuitive map interface. StoreTrail supports multiple store locations with advanced filtering, dealer submission forms for store expansion, and comprehensive analytics to track customer search patterns and store performance.

## Features

- **Interactive Store Locator** with Google Maps integration, customizable search radius, and real-time location-based store discovery
- **Dealer Submission System** that allows authorized dealers to submit new store locations for approval and automatic addition to the locator
- **Choropleth Distribution Map** showing store density by state with interactive state-based filtering and geographic analytics
- **Bulk Import/Export functionality** with CSV support for managing large store networks and data migration
- **Advanced Store Management** with filtering, search, and comprehensive store details including contact information and custom notes
